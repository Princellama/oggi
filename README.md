# Oggi

Eric's daily brief on the web — oggi.princellama.com.

Notion is the store. The morning, afternoon and evening brief runs (Claude) write two Notion tables; this app reads them and writes back what Eric does: notes, intentions, the evening choices, captures. Every edit is also appended to the day's **Edits since brief**, which the next brief run reads and the **Send to Claude** button carries into a new chat.

```
Claude (brief skill) ──writes──▶ Notion: Brief Days + Brief Items ◀──reads / edits── Oggi (this app)
                                  Notion: #QuickActions          ◀──captures, due dates──┘
```

## Views

- **Phone** — three panes you swipe between: **Now** (one thing: the next calendar event, then each open item), **Today** (the brief: terrain, acts, place map, Needs attention, Resolved, intentions), **Capture** (one line, Enter, becomes a QuickAction). After 7 pm, or when the brief's pass is Evening, Today becomes **What moves**: Tomorrow · This week · Let go per item, and one log line. **Read aloud** uses the phone's own voice.
- **Desktop** — the brief on the left, an editor on the right: title, note, place, the evening choice, done, links, capture, and the change list with **Send to Claude**. Keys: `j` `k` move · `t` tomorrow · `w` this week · `x` let go · `d` done · `n` note · `c` capture · `f` focus (Now, full screen) · `r` read aloud · `esc` back.
- **Now: Sort** — the sky's Sort button lays it out three ways, the planets flying to their new places: **Drive** (the place map), **Timeline** (a line across the day, each item at the time it would start, stepping round calendar events) and **By area** (CPA, Medicare, Money and Personal as suns with their items in orbit; Back / Next moves sun by sun). **Tap a sun** (or focus it and press Enter) and the sky goes full screen with that area's card floating over it: a header like *MEDICARE · 2 of 5*, the item, **Done** and Tomorrow · This week · Let go, and a list of everything orbiting that sun. Back / Next and the ← → keys then step through that sun's items only; the other areas dim. Done moves on to the next one in the same sun. Tapping a planet of another area, *Step through every area*, or closing the card (×, Esc, a tap on empty sky) lets the sun go, and if the sun brought the sky full screen, closing the card brings it back. **Sort settings** sets the order (by priority, easiest first, eat that frog, shortest to longest), the start time, breaks, and each item's time and effort; times are guessed from titles (a tax return is two hours) until set. The timeline starts at the current time; optional entries and long blocks such as a port day don't push the work later. **⤢** makes the sky full screen: tapping a planet flies to it and floats its card over the sky (Back / Next inside the card, Esc or a tap on empty sky to close, arrow keys to move). A quiet focus timer sits in the sky's top-right corner, with **Breathe** and **Break** beside it: each opens a frosted, blurred veil over the sky (× or Esc to close). Break counts down the break length with a fresh nudge every 25 seconds (water, stretch, look far away, walk). Breathe offers Energize (4-2-2), Clear (box, 4-4-4-4) and Calm (4-7-8) for 1, 3 or 5 minutes, with an orb that swells on the in-breath and settles on the out-breath. Both end with Start a focus. All of this is kept on the device.
- **Email chip** — an item whose link is a Gmail thread shows a small ✉ EMAIL chip beside its area and place (on Today and on the Now card); tapping it opens the thread. The brief sets the area from the Gmail labels CPA and Medicare.
- **What changed** — when a mail check (or anything else between briefs) adds an item or rewrites one — its title, sentence, status, list, link or hand-off — that planet sparkles in the sky, a **✦ 2 updated** button appears in the sky's bottom-left corner (each tap flies to the next lit planet), and the item carries a **New** or **Updated** tag on its card and on Today. Going to the planet, opening it, or selecting it on Today marks it **read**: it keeps a quiet steady glint (no ripple) and its tag turns to an outlined “New · seen”, fading over its day; only unread ones ripple and count in the ✦ button. An item that changes again after you read it is unread again. Eric's own edits never count, and a new brief pass resets the baseline so a morning brief doesn't light the whole sky. Kept on the device (`seenKeys`, `seenSigs`, `freshKeys`). Beside it, and under the date on Today, a quiet **Updated 2:28 PM · 35 min ago** says when Claude last wrote the day: the later of the brief's **Published** and the mail check's **Mail checked** on Brief Days (hover or long-press says which).
- **Search the sky** — 🔍 beside Sort (or `/` on a computer): type a name, place, area or word from the sentence. Matching planets stay lit, the rest dim, and a list underneath flies to the one you pick (done and let-go planets too).
- **Finished stars** — a job marked done stays in the sky for three days, faded with its name crossed out and how long it took under it (“✓ 4 days”), then leaves. Let-go jobs fade without the line.
- **Star History** — the fourth Sort, with **7d · 1m · 3m** under the Sort button. Every job seen in that window is a comet: its head sits on the day it was done (or today, still open) and its tail reaches back to the day it first appeared, so long tails took long. Done ones are faded and crossed out with “✓ 6 days”, open ones bright with “11 days open”, let-go ones dim. A line of days runs underneath with TODAY on the right; drag left to go back. Notices the brief files straight into Resolved (keys starting `r-`) aren't jobs and stay out.
- **Supernova** — on an item's card (Now card, the full-screen card, the opened item, the desktop side panel), **✹ Supernova** lights the star: it grows one step bigger and brighter every hour, up to 12, until the item is done. **❄ Cool off** shrinks it a step at once and one more every hour until it settles (blue corona while cooling); **Reignite** picks up from its current size. Carried by two dates on Brief Items, **Supernova** (lit at) and **Cooled off**, so it burns the same on every device; each press is a line in Edits since brief.
- **Orbits (parents and children)** — an item whose **Orbits** names another item's Key is that item's child (one level only; Invoices is the first standing parent). Children stay folded out of the sky: the parent carries a dotted ring and a count, and tapping it swings its children out round it on dotted threads (and Back / Next then visits them); tapping it again folds them away. The parent's card says how many are in orbit, a child's card names its parent. Which parents are open is kept on the device (`skyOpenKids`).
- **Waiting on them** — when the ball is with someone outside (a client owes a reply, a document or a choice), *Waiting on them* under an item (or in the opened item and the desktop side panel) asks who, and the item drops out of **Needs me** into a quieter **Waiting on them** band below it, with a grey “⏳ Von · 2d” chip, and out of the Now sequence. *They answered* clears it; *Still waiting* starts the three days again. After three days without a word it comes back to Needs me with the chip turned clay. The brief and the mail check set and clear it from mail. Writes *Waiting on* and *Waiting since* on Brief Items and a line in Edits since brief.
- **People** — an item's **Person** (the client's name, as Eric says it) shows as a small chip; tapping it opens Today gathered to that person, folded children included. A **People** strip above Needs me lists everyone with two or more open items. In the sky, a faint dotted thread joins the open stars about the same person.
- **Decide** — anything carried ten days or more (and not waiting on someone, and not already decided) shows *Carried 23 days. Decide where it goes:* with Tomorrow · This week · Let go, on Today and on the Now card, so it is chosen rather than carried again.
- **Home clock** — when the phone's time differs from home base (Honolulu unless changed in Sort settings), the day line and its stamp carry Honolulu's own day and time, e.g. “Honolulu Fri 3:01 PM”.
- **Invoices** — an item with **Amount** or **Invoice** shows “$750 · To send”, with To send · Sent · Paid to move it along (on Today, the Now card, the opened item and the desktop panel). Paid closes it. A parent whose children are invoices says how much is outstanding.
- **Folding on Today** — a parent's children are folded on Today the same as in the sky: “▸ Show 2 in orbit · $1,150 outstanding” opens them, indented under it; the open/closed state is shared with the sky.
- **Hand off** — on a phone, *Hand off* under an item (on desktop, *Handed to* in the side panel; on the Now card, *Hand it off*) marks it as delegated to Milli, Virgilio or someone else. The item shows a purple “→ Milli · 2d” chip that turns clay after three days; tapping the name again takes it back. Writes *Delegated to* and *Delegated on* on Brief Items and a line in Edits since brief. **Delegate flow** (Eric, 27 Oct 2026): handing to Milli or Virgilio sets *Handoff step* = Queued and opens a panel under the buttons with a notes box (*Handoff notes*, saved as he types) for stream-of-thought instructions. The **delegate** skill, run hourly by the "Oggi hand-offs" scheduled task (or at once from **Send now in Claude**), sends from Eric's own Gmail: the notes become a short email with a numbered checklist, and when the item came in by email the original is **forwarded** underneath with its attachments (Oggi's server can't reach Gmail, so the forward has to come from Claude). It writes back *Handoff emailed*, *Handoff thread* and *Handoff checklist*; the panel then lists the checklist and offers **Ask for status** (step = Ask status; Claude replies in the same thread and sets *Status asked*), **Open the thread** and **Mark done**. The email asks her to reply "Done"; the skill reads replies and either closes the item (step = Done) or sets step = Replied with a one-line *Handoff reply*. Chips read “→ Milli · queued / ✉ 10:52 AM / asked / replied”. In the sky a small moon circles any star someone else has (gold while queued, lilac once sent), with “→ Milli” above it, and the Now card says where it stands. Oggi only ever writes Queued or Ask status; the server rejects other steps. Taking it back clears the step. The old 15-second Resend email is gone.
- **Retrieve Voice Notes / Send Updates** — under *Anything I missed*: opens a Claude chat running the process-captures skill on new Plaud recordings **and** on the lines typed in Anything I missed (the last three days, not yet sent). Once tapped, those lines show “sent to Claude” and don't go twice.
- **Quick asks** — under *Changes since the brief*: **Check email** and **Check hand-offs** open a Claude chat that runs the mail-check skill, a mail-only slice of the brief (logged sent mail, the CPA and Medicare labels, new mail since the last brief or check, hand-offs to Milli and Virgilio).
- **Where you are** (in Sort settings) — **Your time** is Automatic by default (the phone's own zone). When that's wrong, as on ship Wi-Fi or a VPN, pick the place instead (Japan, Korea, Hawaii…): the whole app then runs on that zone (clock, today's date, the timeline, the start time). Oggi also checks the phone's clock against the server's and corrects "now" when it is more than a minute off (a hand-set clock on the wrong zone can be hours out); the focus and break timers keep the phone's own clock. **Remote Work mode** adds a home-base row (Hawaii by default) under each hour on the timeline. Kept on the device.
- **Outbox** — its own tab on phone and desktop: the People and Partners rows whose **Push to** is set and **Pushed** is empty, grouped as TaxDome and Axcess, GHL ready, Partners → GHL, Waiting on information, and Email gaps (folded). Copy one or a batch, **Mark pushed** (writes today into Pushed; Undo clears it), or **Have Claude enter these**, which opens a chat carrying only the Notion row links. Each push is also logged to the day's Edits since brief.
- **Medicare** (`/medicare`, linked from the nav) — Eric's Medicare marketing desk, its own page on the same sign-in. Tabs, each with its own link: **Overview** (tiles for new Pulse items, posts in progress, campaign leads and partners caught in the disruption; the next field days with **Post from the table** on a kiosk day), **Pulse** (Hawaii and national news and carrier moves, filtered by scope and carrier; Mark read, Done with it, Make a post, Archive; **Scan now** opens a Claude chat), **Carriers** (Devoted, UHC, Humana, HMSA: role, stance, 2027 Ready to Sell and their latest Pulse items; below them **Portals and logins**, every site Eric signs in to from the Carrier Portals table: what it's for, a link, the login email or username with Copy, where the password lives, Ready to Sell, Mark checked today. Passwords themselves never go in Notion or the app), **Campaigns** (cost, leads, appointments, enrollments and cost a lead; cost, pieces and status edited in place; add a campaign; **Count from GHL**; field days), **Content** (a new post from a photo and a line, resized on the phone to a 2048px JPEG and uploaded to Notion; Claude writes thumbnail, IG and Facebook captions, summary, call to action and disclaimer; copy buttons; a post flagged *Needs carrier approval* can't be marked posted until a material ID is entered) and **Partners** (the Partners table with Networks, Disruption and Next step; Talked today; a talk-track chat). Top right, under the AEP countdown: **Quote** (Sunfire, IFG) and **Scope** (RetireFlo) buttons. **Carriers** has **2027 Plans** pinned at the top (`/medicare/carriers/2027/Devoted`): each carrier's plans as cards with filters, what changed for 2027, and **▶ Present**, full-screen slides for a kiosk (nicknames never show there; the disclaimer is on every slide). **Veterans** (`/medicare/veterans`): the Oahu MA-only options for TRICARE For Life and VA, a full-screen who-pays diagram, FAQ, a field script, and two emails (educational, or plan-specific for after a Scope). Plan figures for both live in `public/medicare-data.js`, not Notion, since they change once a year. **Activity Log** (`/medicare/log`): every Medicare thing Claude did, from the Medicare Activity Log table, with filters, search, sort and a Details view; the GHL chip (Not in GHL, In Outbox, In GHL) is worked out from the person's People row on each load, never stored. Check people in the log and a bar offers **Claude Quote** (Sunfire IFG: find or add the contact, save as it goes, open the 2027 quote page, leave the Medicare number field for Eric), **Claude Scope** (RetireFlo, one person at a time, plans discussed MA, MAPD, Med Supp, Medicaid, stops at the signature page) and **Claude → GHL** (the ghl-medicare-prospects rules). Each opens a Claude chat whose prompt carries only names, Notion links and GHL contact ids, and asks Claude to write back an Activity Log row, People and a brief item, then message and send a phone notification. Sent rows wear a "With Claude" chip (kept on the device) until a newer log row arrives or six hours pass. The page re-reads Notion every minute, and the main Oggi page every two minutes, while open and idle. Claude's side runs through the `medicare-marketing` skill (scan, count, write, partner). Photos are proxied through `/api/medicare/img/:id` because Notion's file links expire.
- Installable to the home screen; the last brief is cached so it opens on a weak signal.

## FLOWN (/flown)

Eric's FLOWN focus sessions (Tim Longhurst's community), added 27 Oct 2026. Private to him like the rest of Oggi; link in the bottom nav.

- **Rewards** on a night-sky card: a constellation of every finished focus block over the last 14 days (one small constellation per session, deep-work blocks burn gold, today's open blocks wait as dashed rings), the streak (days in a row with a finished block), blocks this week, and focus hours with deep-work hours.
- **A session**: title, host, time, **Join on FLOWN**; the ritual (Breathwork done, his word, the spoken intention); the focus blocks, each with minutes, start time (editable), deep-work flag, an intention, and tasks **pulled from Oggi** (search today's open list). **Start** runs a countdown on the block; **Done** celebrates (chime, burst, "a new star in your sky") and offers "✓ done in Oggi" on each pulled task. At the end, **Gratitude**, then **Celebrate and close** (bigger celebration, Status = Done). Skip a session to drop it from the Timeline.
- **Plan a session**: shapes The Conditions (25, 45, 45 deep, 35 after a 30-min opening, 10-min gaps), Morning Flow (25, 45 deep), or one 50-min deep block. FLOWN events on today's calendar (from the brief) show as "Add it here".
- **Community words**: Eric's word (Role = Me, shown top-right on the page) and each member's word and meaning. Nothing is shared with them.
- **Timeline**: `/api/brief` carries today's sessions as `day.flown`. In the sky's Timeline sort, each open block holds its time: a task pulled into a block sits inside it (split evenly if several), an empty block is a gold FLOWN star, and the rest of the list schedules around the blocks. Pulled tasks carry a "◎ FLOWN 1:05 PM" chip on Today and the Now card.
- **Notion**: FLOWN Sessions (`67565499-243d-4025-8275-61db76180e97`: Name, When range, Host, Join, Word, Intention, Gratitude, Breathwork, Blocks, Status Planned/Done/Skipped, Calendar ID) and FLOWN Members (`92955446-215a-484b-b376-6a3a70721160`: Name, Word, Meaning, Role Me/Host/Member, Since, Note), both under Second Brain. **Blocks** is JSON written by Oggi (`[{n, min, deep, start, intention, items:[{id,key,title}], done, doneAt}]`); a short prefix before the `[` is tolerated. Overrides: `NOTION_FLOWN_SESSIONS_DS`, `NOTION_FLOWN_MEMBERS_DS`. Demo data in `flown-demo.js`.

## Environment variables (Railway → Variables)

| Name | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `NOTION_TOKEN` | The internal integration secret for "oggi". The tables above must be shared with that integration (the four Medicare tables live under the **Medicare Marketing** page, so connecting that page once covers them). |
| `SESSION_SECRET` | Any long random string. Changing it signs everyone out. |
| `ALLOWED_EMAIL` | The address that may sign in (comma-separate more than one). |
| `OGGI_USER` | Username for PIN sign-in (not case-sensitive). |
| `OGGI_PIN` | The PIN. Five wrong tries lock a device out for 15 minutes; fifteen in an hour pause PIN sign-in for an hour. The email link always works as a fallback. |
| `RESEND_API_KEY` | A Resend key for princellama.com (the domain is already verified). |
| `MAIL_FROM` | `Oggi <oggi@princellama.com>` |
| `BASE_URL` | `https://oggi.princellama.com` |

No database. Sign-in is a one-time emailed link (15 minutes), then a 90-day signed cookie.


## Deploy

Same pattern as JIRO and Dolce: Railway web service from GitHub `Princellama/oggi`, custom domain `oggi.princellama.com` via a GoDaddy CNAME plus Railway's TXT verify record.
